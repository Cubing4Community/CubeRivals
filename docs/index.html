How do I find my MAC address?

Turn your cube to wake it, and disconnect it from any other app or device. Then use one of these:

  • Chrome or Edge (computer): type chrome://bluetooth-internals/#devices in the address bar, start a scan and find your cube's name.
  • Android: scan with a Bluetooth scanner app such as nRF Connect.
  • Linux: run bluetoothctl --timeout 15 scan on in a terminal.

The name usually starts with GAN, MG or AiCube. The address looks like AB:12:34:56:78:9A. iPhones, iPads and Macs usually hide it.
